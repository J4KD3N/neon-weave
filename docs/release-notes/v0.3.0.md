# v0.3.0

M3, content complete: the whole campaign, built by `release.yml` for Windows, Linux and macOS from one tag. Placeholder art and audio; the title screen says so.

## What is in it

- **Three acts.** Act 1, The Waking: the plaza, the yard, the Gate Road, Relay Station Nine as the hub with Pell's three lost crews in the Shards, the throat descent in three maps, first contact and the reveal that the Sundering was chosen. Act 2, The Choosing: the oath to one of three factions, the faction's first task, six loyalty quests, the Choir courting in your companions' own voices, three Key delves (one per biome, each with a keeper), and a catastrophe on the plaza shaped by what you did. Act 3, The Loom: the Loom Approach, a door that banks the haul, rival strike teams, the Choir's Voice, and a hand on the Loom.
- **Five endings** that read the hand on the Loom, the Keys you broke, the voice you spared or ended, and every companion's fate, with credits. The Weaver's Mend only on all its conditions.
- **Six companions** with recruit graphs, banter, personal quests that intersect the plot, trust scenes and loyalty quests; a Roster for the bench; both death-stakes paths for every beat.
- **Four romances** on the Quarters, exclusive without jealousy, resolving in Mortal mode.
- **Three factions**, joinable and mutually exclusive, with envoys, areas, vendors, a path quest each, and companion consequences.
- **Ten races** on the creator, each with a mechanical hook that is a rule and gated lines in every act; NPCs react to a Hollow behind you and to a Swarmborn whose disguise slips; skill checks on the creator's attributes.
- **Four biomes** rendering in CI, each with a family, an elite and a boss; the Loom Approach remixes them all.
- **Seven Bastion buildings**: the Archive, the Garden and the Quarters join the four from the demo; account unlocks across playthroughs.
- **Items, affixes and cyberware**; progression to level 12 with multiclassing and capstones; the AP economy of two actions a turn.
- **Modding**: `docs/modding.md`, a validator (`tools/validate_mods.gd`) and an example mod that passes it.
- **The campaign test**: three acts on every faction path in both death-stakes modes, scripted, green in CI.

## Known limits

- Art and audio are generated placeholders.
- Balance is unplaytested beyond the author. The harness holds low floors at depth 3 and for the Key keepers; the Loom's Voice is the hardest fight by design.
- Act 1 is two to three hours, not eight; Acts 2 and 3 are wired, not dense. Approval for loyalty and romance is reachable only slowly.
- macOS is ad-hoc signed and not notarized: right-click → Open, or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine` on the app.
- Steam is wired but unverified against a live client. The demo boundary is `--demo` at launch.

## Running it

| Platform | File | Run |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `neon-weave-v0.3.0-windows.zip` | unzip, run `neon-weave.exe` |
| Linux | `neon-weave-v0.3.0-linux.tar.gz` | untar, `./neon-weave.x86_64` |
| macOS | `neon-weave-v0.3.0-macos.zip` | unzip, right-click → Open the first time |

Saves live under the Godot user data directory (`user://`). Saves from v0.2.0 are refused when their content changed underneath them; start a new game.
